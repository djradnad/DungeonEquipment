Observed level-up costs

One line per upgrade: new level, cost. Values such as 1.60M and 270k are accepted. Apply the data after editing; it is saved only in this browser.

Stat formula settings

The defaults use the level-1 percentages you supplied. A level increase multiplies the displayed stat by its stated level multiplier. Adjust these inputs if a new in-game measurement refines a starting value.

Rank scaling: D–A use 5tier × 1.5rank − 1; S uses 625 × 2rank − 1; SS starts at S4 × 2.5 and uses 2.5 per rank; SSS is SS4 × 12.5. Holding stat 2 starts at S1 and holding stat 3 at SS1.
